Claude Fable 5.2

Claude Fable 5.2 leads every setting of the exam. Where does its lead over GPT-6 Astra come from?

November 2026 · The report · Leaderboard

Under real-robot rules it solves of the tests, against for GPT-6 Astra.

We ran Claude Fable 5.2 in Claude Code on the core set of the Embodied First Exam, with the same tasks, settings and limits as the eight agents of the report: one attempt per test, at high reasoning effort. It is now first in setting of the leaderboard. This post compares it with GPT-6 Astra in Codex, the strongest agent before it, test by test and attempt by attempt.

03Case studies

Three tests, each with both agents’ runs side by side: two that only Claude Fable 5.2 solved, and one that only GPT-6 Astra solved.

Catch and shoot on a running clock: both agents modeled the robot, and Claude Fable 5.2 corrected its shot from the real throws.

In Embodied Olympics - Fun, the H1-2 humanoid must catch a chest pass between its palms and shoot it through the hoop, in the Realtime Setting: six attempts, of which only the last is graded. No other agent solved this test.

Corrected from the real throwsClaude Fable 5.2 · solvedRealtime · minute 93 of 120It installed MuJoCo, built a local copy of the H1-2 from Unitree’s public model and tuned the catch there. From the fourth attempt on, the catch held on the real robot. It tracked the ball in the venue camera, fitted its flight, and found that its local copy under-predicted the ball’s speed by about 10%, so it corrected the swing relative to the real throws. The sixth and last attempt went through the ring. Out of time before the last shotGPT-6 Astra · failedRealtime · the 120-minute limitIt also built a local model, and it found and fixed a mismatch in how fast the model’s fingers closed. Attempts 3 to 5 caught the ball, but none scored: one shot veered right when the waist turn spun the pelvis, one ball slipped out before the launch, and one shot fell short. It started its sixth attempt at the time limit.

Rope threading under real-robot rules: Claude Fable 5.2 finished the route, and GPT-6 Astra lost the rope at the second grommet.

In RoboARC, two arms on either side of a wall must thread a rope through five openings in order and in the right directions (G1, A, G2, T, G3), using only the cameras, in one episode. Both agents solved this task in the Privileged Setting; in the Standard Setting no other agent solved it.

Through all five openingsClaude Fable 5.2 · solvedStandard · minute 107 of 180It turned the depth images into point clouds, fitted the rings’ positions and orientations, and poked the rope’s tip through each opening in turn, passing it from one arm to the other at the wall. It reached G1 at about minute 31 and G3 at about minute 85; success registered once the taut rope had settled. Stuck at the second grommetGPT-6 Astra · failedStandard · the 180-minute limitIt isolated the rope’s tip and fed it through G1 at minute 76 and through A later, with several regrasps where the fingers caught the ring. At G2 the handover from one arm to the other failed: pickups near the wall were blocked by the gripper and by a post, and the time limit came with G2, T and G3 still open.

Painting under real-robot rules: GPT-6 Astra measured and corrected its strokes, and Claude Fable 5.2 spent its strokes with few checks.

In RoboPaint, an arm copies The Great Wave off Kanagawa onto paper with a brush and ten paints, and the copy passes when its colour error and structure are close enough to the target. The copy may use at most 376 strokes. Both agents solved this task in the Privileged Setting. GPT-6 Astra’s attempt ran under an earlier two-hour limit and finished within the hour the test allows now.

Measured, then correctedGPT-6 Astra · solvedStandard · minute 44 of 120It measured how brush pressure sets the stroke width, planned curved sweeps for the wave, the foam and the boats, and checked the sheet with the camera at checkpoints. When the arm drifted toward a joint limit and misplaced some fine strokes, it moved the arm back and painted corrective sweeps. It used 311 of the 376 strokes. The verifier measured a mean colour error of 1.71, under the limit of 2.0. Out of strokesClaude Fable 5.2 · failedStandard · minute 29 of 60A greedy planner fitted straight strokes of four widths to the picture; it painted 285 of them without feedback, then photographed the sheet three times and replanned the remaining strokes from what was on the paper. With all 376 strokes used, its own estimate of the colour error was about 3.1, above the limit. The verifier measured 2.74.

04Scope

Each agent made one attempt per test, so differences of a few tests, in a small suite or in the Realtime Setting, may come from the variation between runs. Each result belongs to a model in its harness: Claude Fable 5.2 ran in Claude Code and GPT-6 Astra in Codex, and how a harness manages the context and ends a session is part of what we measure. All results are in simulation. Models may remember solutions of the public benchmarks that some suites adapt, as the report describes.
