Budgets. Every attempt gets real time and real compute, as an engineer would: from 30 minutes to 4 hours of agent time per task, depending on the suite, and a container with 2 to 16 CPU cores and 4 to 39 GiB of memory. Suites built on GPU simulation backends (Isaac Sim, OmniGibson, SAPIEN) give the agent a GPU, and some tasks in Embodied Olympics - Fun and RoboEngineer offer one for training a policy.
