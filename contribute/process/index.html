Call for Contribution

November 2026

Thank you very much for the earlier discussions, and for your interest in and support of the Embodied First Exam. Several teams have kindly expressed interest in contributing. After learning what each team would like to contribute, we have put together the following shared criteria for your reference.

1. Author list updates

Following the practice of Agent Last Exam, we plan to update the author list every three months.

  • The first version was released in early November, and the paper was accepted to CoRL Late Breaking Results.
  • The second version is planned for around February 2027.

2. Data contributions and suite acceptance

Also following Agent Last Exam, new contributors will join as data contributors. Whether a suite is accepted depends mainly on the following.

Original tasks, kept as a hidden set

We would be grateful if contributed suites were original. These tasks will be used as a hidden set: they will not appear in any public dataset or paper, and models cannot find them on the web, so they measure what models can actually do. Public datasets are crawled regularly by model developers, so results on them may not reflect real ability. For this reason, we are unable to accept public datasets at this time.

Manual review

After a suite is submitted, we will review it by hand and decide whether to accept it, decline it, or ask for revisions. The review uses two criteria models, GPT-6 Astra (high) and Claude Opus 5.5 (high), which may be updated as new models are released. We look at four points:

  1. Challenging: at least one criteria model has a success rate below about 10% in the Standard setting.
  2. Valid: at least one criteria model has a success rate above about 50% in the Privileged setting.
  3. Meaningful: we review each task by hand, based on the task videos and reference outputs you provide.
  4. Quantity: each data contributor contributes one suite of at least 30 tasks.

If you have any questions about these criteria, or would like to discuss task ideas before starting, please feel free to contact us at embodiedfirstexam@gmail.com. Thank you again for your support.
