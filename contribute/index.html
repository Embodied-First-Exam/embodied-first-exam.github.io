Contribute

Call for contributors

The exam is open, and it grows with its community. There are three ways to take part: evaluate a model, integrate an agent harness, or integrate tasks. All are very welcome, from labs, companies and individual researchers alike, and we would love to hear from you: here is how to contribute.

How to contribute

Evaluate a model

Have a new model? We run it on the exam with an existing harness, under the same budgets as every other agent, and its results join the leaderboard.

Get in touch by e-mail →

Integrate a harness

Bring your agent harness to the exam, so that any model can be tested in it and compared with Codex, Claude Code and the other harnesses on the same tests.

Get in touch by e-mail →

Integrate tasks

Add a single task or a whole benchmark suite: frozen scenes, a program that decides success and, where there is one, a reference solution. What a suite needs.

Get in touch by e-mail →

All three go to embodiedfirstexam@gmail.com. For questions, join the exam’s Discord or its .

What a suite needs

  • VerifiableA program decides success, with no learned judge.
  • SolvableFor every family, one answer the verifier marks 1.
  • DifficultHard for today’s code agents, for a robotic reason.
  • ReproducibleA frozen scene that replays bit for bit.
  • IsolatableMarked in its own sandbox from the answer alone.
  • ScrubbableReference solutions kept out of the agent’s reach.
