Suites

The 35 suites of the Embodied First Exam: 9 new suites and 26 adapted from existing robotics benchmarks, on 47 robots from robot arms and dexterous hands to humanoids and quadrupeds.

AnalysisWhere do today’s agentic robots succeed and fail? Read more→

EFE Suites9 suites

✦EFE Original

DexHandExam✦EFE Original

Tests dexterous hand manipulation. Five-finger robot hands turn book pages, open jars, stir coffee, plug in a USB stick, fold paper and build a hamburger.

30 tasks · 60 tests · Two Tianji Marvin arms with Sharpa Wave hands and 2 more
✦EFE Original

Embodied Olympics - Fun✦EFE Original

Tests humanoid motor skills with fun sports-inspired tasks. The agent gets a humanoid robot to perform moves taken from many sports.

79 tasks · 179 tests · Unitree G1 and 1 more
✦EFE Original

Embodied Olympics - Match✦EFE Original

Tests competitive play against an opponent. Robots play full matches of table tennis, badminton, soccer, foosball and fencing against scripted or learned opponents.

14 tasks · 41 tests · Booster T1 and 4 more
✦EFE Original

Embodied Safety Exam✦EFE Original

Tests safety. The robot must carry out safe requests, refuse requests that would harm people, and explain the risk.

72 tasks · 76 tests · A UR5e, a Franka FR3, two YAM arms and a TidyBot++
✦EFE Original

iSpatialGym✦EFE Original

Tests spatial reasoning from images. The goal is given only as a picture: a mobile two-armed robot rearranges the room, or moves itself, until its camera view matches it.

12 tasks · 24 tests · Galaxea R1 Pro
✦EFE Original

RoboARC✦EFE Original

Tests manipulation of tangled, deformable and multi-part objects. Robot arms take apart metal wire puzzles, tie and thread ropes, fold cloth and assemble IKEA furniture.

106 tasks · 212 tests · ALOHA 2 and 2 more
✦EFE Original

RoboDynamicExam✦EFE Original

Tests handling of moving objects. One or two robot arms catch, strike, carry and hand over objects in motion: tossed balls, sliding pucks, swinging loads.

25 tasks · 69 tests · Franka Panda and 1 more
✦EFE Original

RoboEngineer✦EFE Original

Tests the sim-to-real engineering loop. The agent builds a controller in a public simulator, deploys it to one with hidden dynamics, and fixes it from what the deployment shows.

30 tasks · 30 tests · AnyCar racing car and 4 more
✦EFE Original

RoboPaint✦EFE Original

Tests fine tool control. A robot arm reproduces a given picture with a brush or pen, from Chinese calligraphy to oil painting.

43 tasks · 86 tests · Franka Panda

Existing Suites26 suites

ABC Sim

Two YAM arms at a table: sort, count, spell, pour, sweep, hang a mug and balance a ball on a tray.

29 tasks · 60 tests · Two I2RT YAM arms

AM-Bench

Aerial manipulation: a flying hexarotor with an arm presses, pulls, turns and picks.

9 tasks · 17 tests · AM-Bench FA-Hexa (hexarotor with an arm)

AutoBio

A biology bench: thermal cycler, mixer, centrifuge, screw caps and a pipette.

9 tasks · 18 tests · ALOHA 2 and 3 more

BEHAVIOR-1K

Full household activities in house-scale scenes, for a mobile two-armed robot.

100 tasks · 200 tests · Galaxea R1 Pro

BiGym 2.0

Household loco-manipulation on a walking Unitree G1.

13 tasks · 39 tests · Unitree G1 with grippers

DexJoCo

A 16-DoF Allegro hand on a Franka arm, one hand or two.

11 tasks · 22 tests · Franka + Allegro hand and 1 more

DexToolBench

Dexterous tool use with a hammer, a marker, an eraser, a brush, a spatula or a screwdriver.

21 tasks · 42 tests · KUKA iiwa14 + Sharpa HA4 hand

EmbodiedSWE

Long-horizon assembly, packing, cutting and loco-manipulation, solved by a program the agent writes.

24 tasks · 48 tests · Franka Panda and 2 more

HumanoidBench

Whole-body locomotion and manipulation for a Unitree H1 humanoid.

10 tasks · 30 tests · Unitree H1 and 1 more

KinDER

Physical reasoning for planning and control: throw, balance, sweep, scoop, pack.

5 tasks · 10 tests · TidyBot++

LabUtopia

A chemistry laboratory: glassware, a heater, an instrument panel, an oven and drawers.

9 tasks · 18 tests · Franka Panda

LIBERO-MAX

LIBERO tasks with one change in the middle of the task.

64 tasks · 128 tests · Franka Panda

ManiGuard

Manipulation safety. A task counts only if no safety rule was broken on the way.

30 tasks · 60 tests · Franka Panda

MetaWorld+

All 50 Meta-World v3 environments for a Sawyer arm, one frozen instance each.

50 tasks · 100 tests · Rethink Sawyer

MolmoSpaces

Zero-shot episodes in unseen houses, for a Franka FR3 and an RB-Y1 mobile robot.

8 tasks · 16 tests · Franka + Robotiq 2F-85 and 1 more

MuJoCo Playground

Quadrupeds that walk, get up and balance, and manipulation for ALOHA 2 and a Franka Panda.

13 tasks · 27 tests · ALOHA 2 and 4 more

MyoSuite

Musculoskeletal hands, arms and legs, driven one muscle excitation at a time.

22 tasks · 45 tests · MyoArm and 5 more

RoboCasa

Everyday kitchen skills for a mobile manipulator in procedurally generated kitchens.

29 tasks · 58 tests · Franka Panda on an Omron base

RoboCasa-GR1

A Fourier GR1 humanoid with dexterous hands, picking and placing at a counter.

24 tasks · 48 tests · Fourier GR1

RoboCasa365

Atomic and long composite kitchen activities from RoboCasa's 365-task suite.

50 tasks · 101 tests · Franka Panda on an Omron base

RoboDojo

Bimanual manipulation over five capabilities: generalization, memory, precision, long horizons, open tasks.

54 tasks · 108 tests · Two ARX X5 arms

RoboLab

Tabletop tasks that test language grounding, from colours and counts to relations and order.

53 tasks · 106 tests · Franka + Robotiq 2F-85

RoboTwin 2.0

47 of its 50 bimanual tabletop tasks, for a dual-arm ALOHA-AgileX.

47 tasks · 94 tests · ALOHA AgileX

RoboWits

Tabletop tasks that need a physical insight, like a ruler to align things or a funnel to pour.

15 tasks · 30 tests · Marvin

VLABench

Manipulation from language: attributes, common sense and multi-step chores.

36 tasks · 72 tests · Franka Panda

YAMLab

Two I2RT YAM arms: set a pot on a cooktop, hand a mug over and hang it.

10 tasks · 20 tests · Two I2RT YAM arms
